## 音频原文 (Transcript)

```text
Narration: three students are talking about a new lecturer.

Speaker1: What do you guys think about the new lecturer? We’ve had around four classes with him already, and honestly, I really enjoy his teaching style. Now I know why this lecturer is so popular. In the last class I was afraid to speak on class because I might embarrass myself. However, surprisingly, the new lecturer was very nice and gentle, and he did not criticize me at all.

Speaker2: Yeah, I feel the same way. The lecturer is very professional and genuine. Many students asked very difficult questions to challenge him. Even when facing these challenging questions, the lecturer never got annoyed or impatient. Instead, he listened carefully and patiently provided clear, detailed and logical answers to every single question from students.

Speaker1: I have noticed that he even praises students who raise creative or challenging questions. He never feels offended or defensive even when students push back on his original ideas or hold different opinions. His teaching atmosphere is very inclusive and supportive. No one will judge you whether you perform well or make mistakes in his class.

Speaker2: Exactly, I couldn’t agree more. Besides, his academic background is absolutely outstanding and impressive. Whenever students raise questions in class, he always responds quickly and gives accurate and clear answers. Even when students continuously push back or challenge his viewpoints, he always replies in a very calm, patient and professional manner, which is really admirable.

Speaker3: I really like attending his lectures very much, through which I can constantly grow and improve myself both academically and mentally. To be honest, I used to feel really intimidated and stressed in academic lectures. I could hardly fully understand the professional knowledge he explained in class. Therefore, I always preferred to just listen quietly rather than speak up or ask questions actively. I was really afraid of making mistakes and losing face in front of other classmates. Do you guys think I should try my best to become more confident in his future classes?

Speaker1: Honestly, his class is the perfect place to do that. You can both succeed and fail without anyone judging you. We should all try to keep a growth mindset—that’s the only way to really benefit.

Speaker2: True. In fact, in his classes I feel like I’m learning not only academically, but also gaining a lot in terms of personal growth and attitude.

Speaker3: I totally believe that and I’m deeply encouraged. From now on, I will try my best to participate more in classroom interaction. I plan to step out of my comfort zone gradually, speak up bravely and actively improve myself in his future classes.
```
