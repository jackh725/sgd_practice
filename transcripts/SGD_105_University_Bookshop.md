## 音频原文 (Transcript)

```text
Narration: Three students are discussing the university bookshop.

Speaker1: Hey guys, have you heard the university bookstore has closed down? I was totally heartbroken when I found out this morning. I used to stop by it regularly after my morning classes. It's such a shame to lose such a friendly little spot on campus.

Speaker2: Oh no, that's really sad news! But you can find whatever materials you want on the internet, including e-books. They are super convenient, and you can carry hundreds of them on your phone which can be read anywhere. So we don’t have to be too sad.

Speaker3: I just found out yesterday too. Nowadays many students dislike reading, but I still find reading books enjoyable. Reading e-books lacks the same sense that comes from holding a physical book. So, I feel sad. By the way, the bookstore staff were always so approachable.

Speaker1: E-books are better than physical books in so many ways, I have to admit. But your eyes easily get tired after long reading sessions, that's the biggest downside. I really miss the feel of holding a physical book in my hands now. I like to highlight key points on pages and jot down notes in the margins, which gives me the sense of focus.

Speaker2: Anyway, physical bookstores are steadily losing ground, even if you hate to see them go. If you want to prevent bookstores from closing down, you could buy more books.

Speaker3: In the bookstore, the staff were so kind, who could quickly help find textbooks and recommend reference books. It made studying so much easier. Once, I forgot my wallet there, and they kept it safe until I went back for it hours later. That's how lovely the staff were, they really cared about students. The bookstore could have avoided closing if they would have adapted earlier to what students actually need. They were too focused on just selling books alone.

Speaker1: If I had known this bookstore would go out of business, I would have bought far more books here. Really, for the bookstore selling only books is such a narrow focus. Most students go there to borrow books or read them, rather than buy them, so it doesn't really help the bookstore's business. So, they have to diversify, like adding practical items and school supplies, including stationery, tech accessories and daily necessities.

Speaker2: You're totally right, that's such a wasted opportunity for them. If they'd sold more student essentials, it would have brought in way more regular customers.

Speaker3: Exactly, they could have sold phone chargers, notebooks, pens, or other stationery like you said. Thus they could have created a one-stop spot to support students' everyday academic life and brought in more customers. It's such a pity they didn’t adapt in time, the bookstore was such a big part of campus life.
```

## 核心采分点与解析 (Key Points)

*Key points

Topic: university bookshop

主题：大学书店

Speaker One（male）

-Sadness &Past Habits（感到悲伤&过去的习惯）

1.The university bookshop has closed down and he was heartbroken.

提到大学书店已经关门了，他很伤心。

2.He used to stop by the bookshop regularly after morning classes.

他过去常在上午课后顺路去书店。

3.He regrets not having bought more books.

他遗憾于未能多买些书。

-Views on E-books & Physical Books（对电子书和实体书的看法）

1.E-books are convenient but make eyes tired after long reading.

电子书很方便，但长时间阅读会让眼睛疲劳。

2.He misses holding physical books.

他怀念捧着实体书的感觉。

3.Prefer physical books and likes highlighting key points.

提到他喜欢在书上标注重点。

-Suggestion（建议）

The bookshop should have sold practical items.

书店本应该售卖实用物品。

Speaker Two（female）

Sadness & Preference（感到悲伤&偏好）

1.Sadness for the bookshop's closure.

对书店的关闭感到难过。

2.Prefer ebooks

更喜欢电子书籍。

-Praise & Suggestion（赞扬和建议）

Selling more student essentials would have brought more regular customers.

建议售卖更多学生必需品，这样能吸引更多常客。

Speaker Three（male）

Disappointment & Book Preference（感到失望&偏好）

1.The bookshop’s closure is disappointing.

书店关闭令人失望。

2.E-books being handy but physical books unbeatable for deep reading.

电子书很便捷，但实体书在深度阅读方面无可替代。

3.The bookstore staff were kind and helped find textbooks and recommend reference books.

书店工作人员很友善，会帮忙找课本并推荐参考书。

-Reflection on Closure（对闭店的反应）

1. Bookshop could have avoided closing if it adapted earlier.

相信如果书店早些做出调整，本可以避免关闭。

2.The bookshop's narrow focus on only selling books.

书店的经营方向过于狭隘，只售卖书籍。

3.The bookshop's failure to adapt in time is a pity.

书店未能及时适应变化，实在令人惋惜。

>

>

>

>

>

>
> Next, he said that the staff were kind and helped students find textbooks and recommend reference books.
>
> Besides, he pointed out that the bookshop focused too narrowly on selling only books.
>
> So, he came to a conclusion that the bookshop's failure to adapt in time was a pity.
