## 音频原文 (Transcript)

```text
Narration: Three students are chatting in the school library after class about how to improve presentation skills.

Speaker1: Hey guys, I really feel overwhelmed about the ten-minute presentation next week. I've already finished the presentation, but I feel that I didn't do a very good job and I wanna improve. Do you have any suggestions? At the moment, I know that one thing I need to work on is slowing down my speaking speed. I also know that I have a bad habit of constantly looking at my presentation notes.

Speaker2: You're in this situation because you lack practice. So you need practice, practice and practice in front of a mirror—it lets you notice your body language and adjust your tone. Or, you can practice with me. I can be your audience. And one thing I've learned is that you definitely shouldn't read from the script word by word; it makes you sound like a robot and loses people's attention. You say that you keep looking at your notes during the presentation, and I think that’s because your notes are too long. You can cut off some parts and try to look at your notes less.

Speaker3: I agree that you shouldn't read the full transcript. Besides, you should also write down your personal points on the notes. Like, you can highlight the script with pens of different colors for different sections. I believe you can make it easier to understand and memorize your notes, and thus you will depend on them less during the presentation. I used to be nervous, too. One thing that helps me now is I try to avoid looking people’s eyes; instead, I look at their cheeks or chins so it still seems like I'm engaged, which sounds very strange but really works.

Speaker1: Those are really helpful suggestions—thanks, both of you. I've always thought I had to memorize everything perfectly, but focusing on keywords and structure sounds a lot more manageable. Presentation skills are very important for finding a job after graduation, so I take this seriously and I need to practice more.

Speaker2: Absolutely! In fact, presentation skills are useful after graduation, for example, for an interview—you'll need to present yourself confidently under pressure.

Speaker3: Maybe we can set up a small practice session together this week? We could take turns giving feedback and trying out different methods.

Speaker2: Great idea! We could even record each other on our phones—sometimes seeing yourself present helps you notice little things you want to improve. Plus, if we make it a regular thing, we could all become much better speakers by the end of the semester.

Speaker1: I'd really appreciate that. Let's meet tomorrow after school and run through it a few times.
```

## 核心采分点与解析 (Key Points)

*Key points：

Topic: how to improve presentation skills.

主题：如何提升展示技巧

Speaker One (Male)

-Current Situation（现状）

1.He feels overwhelmed about the ten-minute presentation next week.

他对下周的 10 分钟展示感到压力很大。

2.He finished the presentation but wants to improve.

他已经完成了展示内容，但想要进一步提升。

3.He needs to slow down speaking speed and stop looking at notes constantly.

他需要放慢语速，避免频繁看笔记。

-Attitude（态度）

1.Improving presentation skills is a major concern.

提升展示技巧是主要的关注点。

2.He appreciates the suggestions and will meet them tomorrow after school to practice.

他很感谢这些建议，打算明天放学后和大家一起练习。

Speaker Two (Male)

-Practice Suggestions（练习建议）

1.He needs to practice in front of a mirror.

（他）缺乏练习，需要对着镜子练习。

2.He can be Speaker1's audience to practice.

他可以作为第一位发言者的听众，陪他练习。

3.Speaker1 shouldn't read from the script word by word.

第一位发言者不应该逐字读稿子。

4.Cutting off some parts of the notes to look at them less.

建议删减笔记内容，减少看笔记的次数。

-Conclusion（结论）

1.Regular practice can make them better speakers.

规律练习能让他们成为更出色的演讲者。

Speaker Three (Female)

-Note & Delivery Tips（笔记与表达技巧）

1.Agreed with not reading the full transcript.

同意不要逐字读完整稿子。

2.Writing personal points on notes and highlighting with different colors.

在笔记上写下个人要点，并用不同颜色标注重点。

2.Avoids looking people’s eyes and looks at their cheeks or chins.

避免直视对方眼睛，可以看对方的脸颊或下巴。

-Collaborative Practice（协作练习）

1.Taking turns giving feedback and trying different methods.

轮流给出反馈，尝试不同的练习方法。

2.Practicing together helps improve presentation skills.

一起练习有助于提升展示技巧。
