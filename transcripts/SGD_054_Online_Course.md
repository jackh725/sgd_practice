## 音频原文 (Transcript)

```text
Narration: Three students are discussing the pros and cons of online classes, sitting around a small table in the campus cafeteria, their voices mixing with the background chatter of other students.

Speaker1: I really can't stand online classes. The Wi-Fi would also cut out randomly during that economics lecture last week, making the slides freeze constantly. Last week, without teachers' attention, I could hardly concentrate on the class because messages on the phone kept distracting me and I ended up missing half the content.

Speaker2: Actually, I prefer online learning. Think about it - no more rushing to catch the 8 am bus, no spending money on cafeteria food, and I can attend class in my pajamas. I just open my notebook and focus on taking notes. The lack of physical distractions in my own space helps me concentrate better too. The recorded lectures are great too - I can rewatch difficult parts anytime.

Speaker3: But that's exactly the problem! I chose this university for the campus experience. How am I supposed to make friends or network if we're all just little boxes on a screen? And I can't raise my hand to ask questions, so I have to wait until after class. Or, when I'm typing, I get distracted and can't follow the lesson. Even after I type it out, it's basically luck whether the teacher notices it. Sometimes by the time the professor responds, we've already moved on to another topic.

Speaker1: Exactly! It usually takes a few days for me to receive replies from my professor, because he has to respond to so many students' questions by email. In physical classes, I can just raise my hand and get immediate answers. Plus, group discussions are so much more engaging in person. There's no substitute for the energy of debating ideas face-to face, with everyone's gestures and tones adding to the discussion. Online breakout rooms always feel awkward and unproductive.

Speaker2: I get your point about socializing, but consider the flexibility. I can review lecture recordings while eating dinner, or study in my comfortable room instead of those hard library chairs. And I don't have to worry about looking presentable for class, which takes off a lot of social pressure. And honestly, I find I actually participate more online - I'm too shy to speak up in big lecture halls.

Speaker3: That might work for you, but I think face-to-face interaction is an important element. Body language and classroom energy help me understand the material. Last semester I failed a course because the online format just didn't work for my learning style.

Speaker1: Same here! At least in physical classes, the professor can see when students look confused. Online, they just keep lecturing to blank screens. Maybe a hybrid system would be better - some online, some in-person classes? It would balance the need for interaction and flexibility, don't you think?

Speaker2: That could be a good compromise. We'd get the benefits of both formats. Though I'd still prefer fully online - it saves me nearly two hours of commuting every day. Those two hours add up to extra sleep or more time for self - study, which is a huge plus for me.

Speaker3: Well, I'd take hybrid over fully online any day. At least that way I could still get some of the campus experience. But honestly, I think education should be primarily in-person - that's what university is all about.
```

## 核心采分点与解析 (Key Points)

*解析：

Key points:

Topic: pros and cons of online classes

主题：网课的优点和缺点

Speaker 1（female）

-Can't stand online classes.（受不了网课）

1.The Wi-Fi cuts out randomly; getting distracted by phone.

无线网络还会随机断连;被手机分散注意力。

2.Professor sees chat questions next day.

教授第二天才看到聊天框内容。

-Agree with in-person classes（线下课程的优势）.

1.Just raise hand to get answers.

只要举手就能得到答案。

2.Group discussions are more engaging.

线下的小组讨论更具吸引力。

-A hybrid system would be better.

混合教学模式更好。

Speaker 2（male）

-Prefer online learning（更喜欢在线学习）.

1.No 8 am bus rush, no cafeteria costs, can attend in pajamas.

不用再急着赶早上 8 点的公交车，不用花钱买食堂的饭菜，能穿着睡衣上课。

2.Can rewatch difficult parts anytime.

可以随时重看（课程中）难懂的部分。

3.Participate more online.

在网上的参与度其实更高。

4.Hybrid system is a good compromise.

混合教学模式是个不错的折中方案。

Speaker 3 (female)

-Oppose fully online classes（反对纯在线课程）.

1.Want campus experience.

想要校园体验。

2.Learn better through face-to-face interactions

通过面对面互动能学得更好。

- Take hybrid over fully online any day.

无论何时，都更愿意选混合教学模式，而不是纯在线课程。

-Education should be primarily in-person.

教育本该以线下形式为主。
