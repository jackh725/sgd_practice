## 音频原文 (Transcript)

```text
Narration: Two students and one librarian are having a discussion in the library, focusing on the noise problem, rule enforcement and suggestions for improving the learning environment.

Speaker1: The library is supposed to be a quiet, peaceful place for people to focus on their studies, finish assignments, or prepare for exams. But recently, many students in the library have been talking loudly, laughing, and even chatting freely with their friends, making it feel more like a busy social club than a place for academic work.

Speaker2: I am well aware of this situation, and I completely understand your frustration. However, their noisy behavior is quite difficult for us to manage effectively. As you can see, our library is quite large, with multiple floors, reading areas, and public spaces, and we simply do not have enough staff to monitor every corner and every student’s behavior at all times. Perhaps you could consider moving to the first floor or the computer area, where the atmosphere is a bit more relaxed.

Speaker3: Unfortunately, the computer area isn’t any better. Many students there watch sports games, entertainment videos, or even live streams on their computers, and they often play the audio very loudly without using headphones. On top of that, their conversations are also quite noisy. All this noise makes it just as hard for us to concentrate on our work as the main reading area, if not harder.

Speaker1: I see what you mean. Maybe a clearer division of areas would help. Perhaps it would be more effective to clearly state the rules. For example, small talking should be allowed in the computer area. Meanwhile, in the main reading area and silent study rooms, people should try not to talk at all, not even in a low voice. This way, students will know exactly where it is acceptable to talk.

Speaker2: Actually, talking is already allowed in our computer area. As for the silent area, if you ever feel distracted by noise there, you can call the security desk directly for help.

Speaker1: I think the library should strengthen its promotion of these rules.

Speaker2: That’s a very good suggestion. You’d better write down your complaints and suggestions in detail. The university attaches great importance to students’ opinions.

Speaker3: That’s good to know, but why do some people bring drinks into the library? Is that permitted? I’ve seen students spill their drinks on the tables and chairs several times, leaving stains that are hard to clean.

Speaker2: Bringing drinks into the library is actually not allowed. Drinks can easily be spilled, making the library environment messy and untidy, and they can also cause damage to books, tables, and other public facilities.
```

## 核心采分点与解析 (Key Points)

Key points

Topic : library noise problem

主题：图书馆的噪音问题

Speaker one （female）

-Attitude 1: The library should be a quiet and peaceful place for study.

态度 1：图书馆应该是一个安静平和的学习场所。

-Examples:（例子）

1.Recently many students are talking loudly.

最近很多学生在大声交谈。

2.The noisy situation in the library makes it like a social club.

图书馆里的嘈杂环境让它像一个社交俱乐部。

3.The noise has seriously influenced students’ learning and concentration.

噪音严重影响了学生的学习和专注力。

-Attitude 2: The library should strengthen the promotion of the rules.

态度 2：图书馆应该加强规则的宣传。

Speaker Two（male)

-Attitude 1: The library has difficulties in managing noise and related rules.

态度 1：图书馆在管理噪音及相关规则方面存在困难。

-Examples:（例子）

1.The noisy behavior is hard to manage effectively.

这种吵闹行为很难有效管理。

2.Talking is already allowed in the computer area.

电脑区域已经允许交谈。

-Attitude 2: Students can call the security desk for help.

态度 2：学生可以向安保台求助。

-Attitude 3: The university values students’ opinions.

态度 3：学校重视学生的意见。

Speaker Three（female)

-Attitude 1: Agreed with the first speaker about the serious noise problem.

态度 1：同意第一位发言者关于噪音问题严重的观点。

-Examples:（例子）

1.The terrible situation in the computer area.

电脑区域的糟糕状况。

2.Students watch videos and play loud audio without headphones in the computer area.

学生在电脑区域不戴耳机看视频、播放音量大的音频。

3.Some students bring drinks into the library.

部分学生将饮品带入图书馆。

-Attitude 2: Improving the library environment with proper rules.

态度 2：通过合理的规则可以改善图书馆环境。

答案：

The discussion involves three people talking about library noise problem.

The speaker one first mentioned that the library should be a quiet and peaceful place for study.

And then, the speaker has explained that recently many students are talking loudly.

Followed by that, in the discussion, she mentioned the noisy situation in the library makes it like a social club.

From her, we know that the noise has seriously influenced students’ learning and concentration.

In the end, the speaker said the library should strengthen the promotion of the rules.

The second speaker is mainly about the library’s difficulties in managing noise and related rules.

Firstly, the speaker emphasized that the noisy behavior is hard to manage effectively.

He also mentioned that talking is already allowed in the computer area.

Lastly, the speaker believed that students can call the security desk for help.

As a result, he finally concluded that the university values students’ opinions.

As for the third speaker, she agreed with the first speaker about the serious noise problem.

What's more, she shared the opinion about the terrible situation in the computer area.

Next, she had faith in improving the library environment with proper rules.

After that, she claimed that students watch videos and play loud audio without headphones in the computer area.

Besides, she highlighted the point of the problem that some students bring drinks into the library.

Overall, they focused on the main topic of the library noise problem.
